About the apartment

Elegance and comfort in the heart of Subotica

Apartman Mirakul is located in the iconic Prokeš Palace — one of the most beautiful buildings in the center of Subotica, dating back to the era when Art Nouveau shaped the city's unique character. The blend of historic charm and modern comfort creates a special atmosphere from the very first moment.

What guests appreciate most is the location — nearly everything worth experiencing in Subotica is just a short walk away. City Hall, the Korzo promenade, restaurants, cafés, the synagogue, and many of the city's landmarks are all within easy reach, with no need for a car.

The apartment has been carefully designed to make you feel relaxed and comfortable throughout your stay. A fully equipped kitchen, air conditioning, Smart TV, high-speed Wi-Fi, and thoughtful details that make you feel at home are all at your disposal.

Whether you are visiting for a weekend getaway, a business trip, or simply to experience the charm of Subotica from one of its finest locations — Mirakul is a place where comfort, atmosphere, and the city come together in one memorable stay.
